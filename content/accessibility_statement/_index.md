#### **Accessibility Statement**

This accessibility statement applies to [SciLifeLab Data Platform](https://data.scilifelab.se/), a service provided by [SciLifeLab Data Centre](https://www.scilifelab.se/contact/data-centre/).

SciLifeLab Data Centre is committed to ensuring digital accessibility for all users. We continuously work to improve the accessibility and usability of our digital services while identifying and removing barriers to access.

This accessibility statement describes the accessibility of this service, outlines any known accessibility issues, and explains how users can contact us if they encounter accessibility-related problems.

##### **How accessible is the service?**

This service is fully compliant with the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA.

##### **What to do if you cannot use parts of the service**

If you need information from this service in an alternative format, or if you encounter accessibility barriers, please contact us by sending an email to [data-platform@scilifelab.se](mailto:data-platform@scilifelab.se).

##### **Report accessibility problems**

We continuously work to improve accessibility.

If you discover accessibility problems that are not described in this statement, please let us know.

Please include:

- the page, document, or feature affected
- a description of the issue
- your browser, device, or assistive technology, if relevant

Contact: [data-platform@scilifelab.se](mailto:data-platform@scilifelab.se)

##### **Report deficiencies in accessibility**

The Agency for Digital Government (DIGG) is responsible for supervising compliance with the Act on Accessibility to Digital Public Services. If you are not satisfied with how we handle your feedback regarding accessibility, you can contact DIGG and report deficiencies in the accessibility of this service [here (https://www.digg.se/tdosanmalan)](https://www.digg.se/tdosanmalan).

##### **Technical information about accessibility**
###### **Compliance status**

This service is fully compliant with applicable accessibility requirements.

###### **Inaccessible content**

There aren't any inaccessible content within this service at the moment that we are aware of. Please contact us: [data-platform@scilifelab.se](mailto:data-platform@scilifelab.se) to report if you encounter any.

##### **How accessibility was assessed**

We use continuous self-assessment to determine the accessibility of the site. This is done both manually, and using tools such as [WAVE](https://wave.webaim.org/) and [Pa11y](https://pa11y.org/). The site is tested on different browsers. User feedback is also sought regularly to ensure accessibility.

- Most recent assessment: 23rd August 2026
- Assessment carried out by: [SciLifeLab Data Centre](https://www.scilifelab.se/contact/data-centre/)
