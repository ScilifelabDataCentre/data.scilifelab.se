Data.scilifelab.se is undergoing multiple updates to improve the usability of the site. The new [Guidance section](/guidance/) aims to collate sources of information to users interested in topics related to supporting research efforts. This includes, for example, data management (including all phases of the data lifecycle), and access to services from SciLifeLab Data Centre.

The [guidance section](/guidance/) replaces the previous 'resource' section. The aim is to make it easier for users to locate information that could aid their research efforts.

If you feel that there is a topic that you would like further guidance on, please [contact us](/contact/). SciLifeLab Data Centre staff will assess any requests, and create guidance/provide information as needed.
