SciLifeLab is dedicated to facilitating collaboration, driving innovation, and empowering the
scientific community through Open Science practices and the FAIR principles. SciLifeLab has multiple initiatives that focus on creating impactful, researcher-driven solutions to enhance data sharing, discovery, and reuse across life sciences. SciLifeLab is also proud to collaborate with others on other initiatives that align with our commitment to Open Science, FAIR principles, and the sustainable development of research infrastructure.

This page lists both SciLifeLab initiatives and external initiatives supported by SciLifeLab (termed 'collaborative initiatives').
