## SciLifeLab AI Network

Through the AI Network, SciLifeLab endeavors to provide a community space for anyone involved in life science to share experiences and get peer support for using and developing AI tools. The community is hosted on the SciLifeLab Slack workspace, and hosts monthly seminar series and discussion groups. 

The AI network targets multiple stakeholders:

* <b>Community members</b> are provided a space to to discuss AI tools informally, learn, share tips and get help from peers.

* <b>Developers</b> can ask for feedback, find pilot users, and make their tools visible to the community. The network also enables AI tool developers to find each other, exchange practical development tips, and start collaborations. In short, the network provides a focused space for people who build tools to talk to each other, distinct from the general AI discussion space meant for all users.

* <b>Members of SciLifeLab management</b>, are able to access an information channel for understanding what the community needs and is interested in, so future AI development and support can be driven by real use cases.

### Get involved

Members of the SciLifeLab Slack workspace can join the #ai-network channel.

Those not affiliated with SciLifeLab can request access by [emailing the AI network](mailto::ai-network@scilifelab.se).

### Meetings/Events

The network organises two types of meetings: AI Network discussions, and a public AI Seminar Series.

#### AI Network Discussions

The AI Network discussions comprise an informal community-driven forum for exchanging practical AI knowledge in Life Science; what works, what doesn't, and what's emerging. Discussions are informal and on topics suggested and led by members and open to anyone. Sessions typically comprise of ~10–30 minute presentations followed by a discussion. The sessions are announced on the #ai-network Slack channel.

The discussions are typically held on [Zoom](http://meet.nbis.se/ainw) at 10am(CET/CEST) on the first Friday of the month during academic terms (with some exceptions).

#### SciLifeLab AI Seminar Series

The AI Seminar Series hosts monthly talks at the intersection of AI and Life Science. Speakers from academia and industry present on topics ranging from deep learning and integrative omics to AI agents, computational drug discovery, to clinical Natural Language Processing (NLP). Any topic where AI meets biology is a possiblity. Seminars comprise of a ~45 min presentation followed by 15 min discussions. Anyone is free to suggest future speakers.

The AI Seminar Series typically runs on [Zoom](http://meet.nbis.se/ainw) at 10am(CET/CEST) on the third Friday of the month during academic terms (with some exceptions).

Check out the dedicated [AI Seminar Series page on scilifelab.se](https://www.scilifelab.se/research/scilifelab-seminar-series/ai-seminar-series/) to read more, find out the current schedule, and suggest future speakers.
