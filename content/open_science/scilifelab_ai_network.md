## SciLifeLab AI Network

The SciLifeLab AI Network is a community for researchers, technical staff, and developers
exploring AI in life science research. Through Slack discussions, seminars, and discussion
groups, members can share experiences, exchange practical knowledge, get peer support, and
connect with others across disciplines.

The network is part of the SciLifeLab AI strategy and is organised by NBIS and SciLifeLab Data Centre.

**Why join the AI Network?**

* **For community members:** Discuss AI tools, share tips, and learn from peers working with AI
  in life science research.

* **For developers:** Get feedback, find pilot users, and make your tools visible to the
  community. The network also provides opportunities for AI tool developers to connect,
  exchange practical development tips, and start collaborations with others building tools
  and workflows.

* **For SciLifeLab leadership and management:** Gain insight into community needs, interests,
  and real use cases to help guide future AI development and support.

### Get involved

The network's main communication channel is
[#ai-network](https://scilifelab.slack.com/archives/C040PHK50KD) on the SciLifeLab Slack
workspace, where events are announced and members share and discuss AI-related news and projects.

If you are not affiliated with SciLifeLab and want to join our Slack, or if you want to receive
email notifications about the seminars, contact us at
[ai-network@scilifelab.se](mailto:ai-network@scilifelab.se).

### Meetings/Events

The network organises two types of meetings: AI Network discussions, and a public AI Seminar
Series. All meetings are hosted on [Zoom](http://meet.nbis.se/ainw).

#### SciLifeLab AI Seminar Series

The AI Seminar Series hosts monthly talks from speakers working with AI in Life Science, both
from academia and industry. Topics range from deep learning and integrative omics to AI agents,
computational drug discovery, and clinical NLP, highlighting current developments where AI meets
biology. Community members are welcome to suggest future speakers and topics.

The AI Seminar Series typically runs at 10am (CET/CEST) on the third Friday of the month during
academic terms (with some exceptions).

View the schedule, suggest future speakers, and read more on the
[AI Seminar Series page on scilifelab.se](https://www.scilifelab.se/research/scilifelab-seminar-series/ai-seminar-series/).

#### AI Network Discussions

The AI Network Discussions are informal, community-driven sessions for exchanging practical AI
knowledge in Life Science research: what works, what does not, and what is emerging. Sessions
are based on topics suggested and led by community members, with opportunities for discussion
and questions.

The discussions are typically held at 10am (CET/CEST) on the first Friday of the month during
academic terms (with some exceptions).

Upcoming discussions are announced in the Slack channel, #ai-network, and through the
mailing list.
