Data services are defined as software produced to help with at least some element of data management. This could include, for example, communication tools used to enable collaboration to handle data, or a tool used to share, analyse, or store data. This site provides information about SciLifeLab Data Services, including what data services are available from SciLifeLab, and information relevant to those services.

This site is developed and maintained by SciLifeLab Data Centre. Content is also provided by [National Bioinformatics Infrastructure Sweden (NBIS)](https://nbis.se), [SciLifeLab Training Hub](https://training.scilifelab.se), and the general SciLifeLab community.

The site is funded by the [SciLifeLab & Wallenberg National Program for Data-Driven Life Science (DDLS)](https://www.scilifelab.se/data-driven) and the [Swedish Foundation for Strategic Research (SSF)](https://strategiska.se/en/).

Feedback on any part of the SciLifeLab Data Services site is always welcomed, and can be provided via the [contact page](/contact/).

### History of the site

This site was operated as the SciLifeLab Data Platform between June 2022 and December 2026. References to 'SciLifeLab Data Platform' remain on the site in content where deemed appropriate. This includes, for example, in news items and articles produces during the operation of the website under that name.
